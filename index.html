Statements

Two statements, the same eight sections in the same order. One gives you the totals; the other gives you every line behind them.

Concise statement
3 pages

The totals. What you hold and what it is worth, how each investment performed, what you bought and sold, the income you received, your wallets and what you paid us.

  • One block per section
  • Holdings with units, weight, price and value
  • One return percentage and one money amount per investment
Detailed statement
7 pages

Every entry behind those totals — the document to reconcile against, or to hand to an accountant, a lender or an auditor.

  • Every holding with its ISIN, exchange, cost and profit and loss
  • Every order, distribution, fee and wallet movement
  • How each return was worked out, and a glossary

Conventions

So the app and the statement can never disagree by rounding.

PercentagesOne decimal everywhere.Settled
CurrencyBalances, values and totals in whole units. Prices, per-unit costs, income and fees keep their cents — as does anything under 10.Settled
Display FX rateShown as USDZAR 18.42; source and timestamp in the tooltip.Settled
Converted figuresValues only. Never a blended cross-currency return.Settled
Rounding orderConvert at account level so groups sum to Total value.Settled
Dealt rates keep full precisionArithmetic, not preference. R13,271.76 at 16.5897 gives exactly $800; at 16.59 it gives $799.99 and the statement stops reconciling.Confirm
Where the cents line fallsA share price and a distribution keep cents; a portfolio balance does not. The boundary is the kind of figure, not its size — worth confirming it reads that way to a client.Confirm
Fee rates at one decimalAdvice 0.5% (from 0.51%), custody 0.1% (from 0.14%). These are disclosed rates; one decimal understates custody by a third.Confirm
One position, one goalSplitting a holding across goals makes the row chip ambiguous.Confirm
